Pools

A pool is one model and how it is served. Each replica serves all of it: split over its devices, on one console by itself, on this PC's GPU, or a server elsewhere. Requests go to the pool their "model" names.

Devices

Consoles switch between worker mode and the app from here; a running pool's devices are its own.
